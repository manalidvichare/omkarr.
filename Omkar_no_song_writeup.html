We fought about it, and it felt like a promise had been broken. I don't want to pretend it didn't matter to me. I want us to be able to talk honestly about what happened, what boundaries mean to both of us, and how trust can be rebuilt through actions.

The situation involving Saanvi made me angry, and I let that anger decide how I treated your birthday. I didn't wish you, and I know you were alone. My love wasn't missing, but I understand that my actions may have made you feel forgotten. I'm sorry for that.

02 · From my heart

What I wish I could explain better

Hey Omki,

I love you so much. But I know I don't always show it in the right way. I overthink, I get upset, and sometimes I react before I understand what you're trying to say. I'm not making excuses. I know I need to work on that, and I really want to.

What happened at the villa hurt me. Seeing those photos and videos made me feel unsure and upset. I want you to understand why it affected me. I don't want us to keep fighting about it; I want us to talk honestly and be clear about what feels okay for both of us.

About your birthday — I was upset and let that feeling decide what I did. I didn't wish you, and I'm sorry. I know that must have hurt. You matter to me, and I should have shown you that, especially on your birthday.

When I talk about our relationship and our future, it is because I think seriously about us. I think about what could become difficult later. I know my parents and how important it is to me that, if we reach that stage, they can accept you and the future we choose. I want both of us to have good careers, stand on our own feet and build a secure, happy life. I want to be able to proudly say that we built something healthy together. That is my dream, not a pressure I want to put only on you.

I care about us, and I want this to work in a healthy way. I want us to trust each other, speak calmly, and make space for both our feelings. I'll try to listen better and explain what's hurting me without turning it into a fight. I hope we can both do that for each other.

We may not agree on everything, and that's okay. I just want us to learn from our mistakes, be kinder during hard moments, and remember that we're on the same team.

Thank you for being you, and for being part of my life. I love you. I hope I keep showing it better, every day.

With love,
Your girl ♡
